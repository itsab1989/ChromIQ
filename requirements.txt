# CR30 instrument support (#159). The vendored driver in workflow/cr30 talks to
# the instrument directly — ChromIQ reads a CR30 itself and feeds the values to
# chartread with -x, so Argyll never opens it.
#   pyserial — USB, the CH34x bridge at /dev/cu.usbserial-* (no driver needed on
#              macOS: Apple ships AppleUSBCHCOM)
#   bleak    — Bluetooth LE, cross-platform and driver-free

# without them the Measure tab cannot open the instrument at all and the log
# says only "no USB device (No module named 'serial')".
